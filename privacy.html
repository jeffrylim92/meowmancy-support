PRIVACY POLICY

Your adventure stays on your device.

Effective October 15, 2026 · Bundle ID: com.meowmancy.game.ios

This Privacy Policy explains how the current version of Meowmancy handles information. Meowmancy is an offline single-player game and does not currently require an account.

Information we collect

Meowmancy does not currently collect or transmit personal information, gameplay analytics, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or diagnostic logs to the developer.

Information stored on your device

The game stores progress and preferences locally, including campaign records, unlocked cats, upgrades, achievements, settings, Daily Challenge records, and supported suspended-run data. This information is used only to operate the game on your device.

Daily Challenges

Daily Challenges use the device’s UTC date to generate deterministic content. The date is processed locally and is not sent to a Meowmancy server.

Backups and deletion

Apple’s device backup services may include app data according to your device and backup settings. Meowmancy does not control those services. Deleting the app removes its local container from the device, although a separate device backup may retain a copy under Apple’s policies and your settings.

Purchases and advertising

The current version does not include in-app purchases, third-party advertising, or advertising tracking. If these features are introduced, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before the applicable release.

Third-party services

Installing or updating the app through the App Store is subject to Apple’s own privacy practices. Following a link to GitHub support is subject to GitHub’s privacy practices. Those services operate independently from Meowmancy.

Children’s privacy

Meowmancy does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other players. The app does not include chat, user-generated content, account creation, or social features.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game’s features or data practices change. The latest version will remain available on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, open a support request in the Meowmancy support repository. Do not include sensitive personal information, receipts, passwords, or device identifiers in a public issue.
